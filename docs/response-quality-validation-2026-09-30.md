## Semantic parsing without a generative model: proposed experiment, October 1

The user requested a cheaper semantic-parsing method. The following records the proposal before the experiment above. Independent symbolic and discriminative research plus an Astra design review support **one bounded experiment with a typed domain grammar and selective Jev ambiguity resolution**. The grammar experiment is now implemented and fails its quality gates; selective Jev resolution remains untested. The earlier generative extractor suggestion remains an alternative; these results do not establish its necessity.

Use offset-preserving normalization and a token/entity lattice, then compositional grammar rules that produce a typed constraint tree and explicit state operations. A budget node consumes its amount, comparison and basis together; `maximum` cannot remain an independent topic. A negation node owns its coordinated category group; modality belongs to the affected subject. Keep source spans, unknown material and materially different readings. Apply corrections through deterministic `Set`/`Remove`/`Replace`/`Reset` operations only after validating the selected tree. Avoid inferring a budget basis, quality, exact party size or accessibility from an unrelated companion or category mention.

[Nearley](https://nearley.js.org/) is an MIT JavaScript Earley parser supporting ambiguous parses; its [semantic postprocessors](https://nearley.js.org/docs/grammar) can build structured nodes. It is a candidate for the local prototype, not a Turkish semantic model or a performance guarantee. Hierarchical meaning is warranted: [task-oriented semantic-parsing research](https://aclanthology.org/D18-1300/) documents compositional requests that flat intent/slot representations cannot faithfully express. Turkish morphology/normalization may help inflection and noisy text; [Zemberek](https://github.com/ahmetaa/zemberek-nlp) provides these functions but has Java/runtime and slow-maintenance costs. Do not add a morphology service without evidence of the missing family. Generic dependency arcs do not establish domain semantics.

A unique parse alone is insufficient: it may be the only reading an incomplete grammar supports. Require explicit accounting for decision-relevant text, valid bindings and consistent transitions. Cap token count, chart work and materially distinct alternatives; preserve diagnostics when a cap is reached. Only represented ambiguous bindings may use Jev [Choice](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives/choice) over fully described alternatives, including none. Missing alternatives or unsupported material require clarification. This differs from the failed independent field questions: the selected object preserves its internal value/operator/scope relationships. Unknown substantive fragments cannot be swallowed as filler or silently become optional topics. Quote/offset validity is provenance, not semantic truth.

Freeze grammar and policies before one independent benchmark of 200 cases (100 Turkish, 100 English): 120 clear supported, 40 ambiguous, 40 unsupported/incomplete, with at least 40 multi-turn cases included in those totals. Retain original failures as a separate regression cohort. Label source bindings, full trees, state operations and expected clarification. Proposed gates: automatic acceptance of at least 90% of clear requests, material tree accuracy of at least 98% among those accepted, zero observed silently weakened hard constraints or forced unsupported guesses, and correct alternatives represented in at least 95% of ambiguous cases. Record exact counts by family; refusing everything cannot pass. Parser-only warm p95 target is 25 ms on named hardware, with model-call avoidance, Jev latency/cost, false refusals and hosting measured separately. These are proposed gates, not passing evidence or reliability guarantees. Stop and discuss missing generalization or ambiguity explosion instead of tuning against failed holdouts.

Rules have no token/API fee on their deterministic path, but compute and grammar maintenance remain costs. A supervised multilingual span/relation/transition encoder is another non-generative option, requiring reviewed domain annotations and learning curves; the current tiny diagnostics do not justify training readiness. No model training, package installation, paid calls, production edits or deployment occur during this research.

## Defects identified in the initial audit

1. **Required topic versus optional preference.** The current state stores arbitrary interests as optional, while mandatory requirements use a closed vocabulary. “Photography-related, preferably a workshop” cannot faithfully require photography while leaving workshop optional. The ranker explicitly treats the mandatory list as complete. 2. **Optional geography and correction recovery.** The interpreter unconditionally rejects `Anadolu yakası` / `Avrupa yakası`, even in an optional preference. Pending text can keep blocking a later precise Kadıköy correction. Unsupported optional wishes must not invalidate supported mandatory conditions.

These are demonstrated fallback defects, not evidence that today's Jev makes the same decisions. Fallback must retain recognized hard conditions and clarify unresolved ones before returning cards.
4. **Attendance evidence and main-program relevance.** Historical after-work results included a timed 10:30 experience and entries with unresolved admission/opening hours. Ask for the user's earliest arrival where needed and require compatible attendance evidence. A performer biography, genre overlap or generic entertainment does not establish the requested program or experience.
